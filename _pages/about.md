Greetings Mortals...

to my page!

I possess a Theoretical Physics Master's degree from The University of Manchester, and I am currently being employed as an R&D Physicist at a Semiconductor Metrology company. Despite this, I'm open to a versatile selection of activities including (the most boring one) new opportunities for professional occupation. Besides my day job (which currently solely serves as an income source, although if an opportunity exciting enough emerges I would love to call it my work) I work on several things. I'm currently writing a computer simulation of a Formula One race, using stochastic models. If interested you can read more on that under the <a href="https://janosdeak.github.io/projects/"> projects</a> tab. My other activity is writing, mostly fictional short stories in the genre of science fiction. Despite that, currently I am working on a non-fiction novel about the life of the Hungarian youth in Slovakia, which is mostly based on mine and my peers' experience during our Gymnasium years. The mood of the work reflects on the reality shift from mafia-driven post-soviet Eastern Europe towards the American influenced Western European "idol". At the moment all my fictional work is hidden behind, for most, an unsurpassable and undecipherable wall of code called the Hungarian language. However, English speaking people do not worry! In some time, translations will emerge of the worthy materials. Also, as a side hobby I'm working towards developing my English skills up to a point where I can comfortably read classical literature and write eloquently.
