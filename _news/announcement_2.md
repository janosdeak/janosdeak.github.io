This September was the second time this year (and overall) that I got published. As the title says, this time my short story was accompanied by an interview as well, which is located on the final page. The magazine is called Levél Kedvesemnek and it is a small and independently run Hungarian literary magazine based in Slovakia. The main guy behind it is the founder and chief editor Richárd Saláth. His and a few others' honest work allows the opportunity for young or not-so-young beginner writers to get published. Not *"just"* online, but in **print** as well. That might not be such a great accomplishment to many people, but personally, for me that is a huge triumph. Of course I am still an amateur writer, by that I mean that I have earned a grand total of *zero* shiny pebbles in any form from writing. Becoming a professional writer however doesn't mean that someone becomes a good or even a decent writer. The latter I would consider as being one of my inspirations in life. I want to be able to tell stories, convey emotions, spark thoughts and disruption in people's minds and souls. I want the ability to be able to express what's inside my own mind as vividly and realistically as possible.
